# Product-Sales-Dashboard
An interactive dashboard that analyzes product performance across categories and sub-categories. It highlights key KPIs, top-performing products, discount impact, shipment distribution, and monthly sales trends
The objective of the project was to provide clear insights into sales trends, profitability, product demand, and the impact discounts on quantity sold
 to support data-driven decision-making. 

•​Key Performance Indicators (KPIs):
-​Number of Products: 1,862
-​Total Quantity Sold: 37,873
-​Number of Categories: 3
-​Number of Sub-Categories: 17
-​Grand Total Sales: $2,297,201
-​Grand Total Profit: $286,397

•​Product & Category Analysis:
-​Top 10 Products by Sales vs. Profit: Identifies high-performing items like the Canon imageCLASS 2200, which leads in both sales and profit.

-​Category Insights: Breaks down performance across Furniture, Office Supplies, and Technology, showing Phones and Chairs as top revenue drivers.

-​Monthly Product Trends: Tracks the volume of products sold monthly, peaking in November (1,021) and December (971).

•​Logistics & Discount Impact:
-​Shipping Performance: Shows that Standard Class accounts for the largest share of products sold (40% or 1,774 units).

-​Quantity vs. Discount: Analyzes how promotional discounts influence the sales volume of the top 10 products.
